About me

I'm a freshman at UCCS studying Data Analytics and Systems Engineering, and I compete for the Mountain Lions as an NCAA Division II track athlete.

I've been working since third grade. Six years of raising and selling livestock through 4-H taught me to run something like a small business: daily care, costs, and selling at the fair. In high school I balanced a full course load, sports year-round and a part-time job, and still finished with a 4.0 unweighted GPA.

My jobs in window repair and HVAC taught me how real systems fail, how to troubleshoot them methodically, and how to explain the fix to the customer standing next to me. As track captain I learned to lead: running warm-ups, mentoring younger athletes and leading practices on my own.

I'm looking for internships where I can use data to solve practical, operational problems.
